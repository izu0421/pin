The one-sentence story

In CIDP blood, autoreactive effector T cells and interferon-activated monocytes are exiting the circulation for peripheral nerve tissue via a CX3CR1–fractalkine axis, while TREM1-driven inflammatory monocytes and immunosuppressive neutrophils accumulate in the blood as a counter-regulatory response.

Interpretation. The pattern — effector T cells and IFN monocytes leaving the blood, TREM1⁺ inflammatory monocytes and LDGs staying behind — is the signature of an active, compartmentalized neuroinflammation with the target tissue (peripheral nerve) siphoning off the effector arm while the counter-regulatory arm remains in circulation.

Two therapeutic entry points

1 · Block the CX3CR1–fractalkine axis

CX3CR1 is upregulated across the depleted, migrating populations — CD8 CTL/EM, NK, CD14⁺ monocytes, CD16⁺ monocytes. Small-molecule antagonists and anti-CX3CL1 antibodies exist in clinical-stage development.

2 · Target TREM1⁺ inflammatory monocytes

DPYD⁺PLXDC2⁺ monocytes carry the strongest activation signature (TREM1 pathway NES = +2.18) and are the likely source of circulating IL-6, IL-8 and IL1RN. TREM1 inhibitors are in Phase II development for sepsis.

Terminal effector CD8 T cells are leaving the blood

150 of 2,200 T CD8 CTL/EM neighborhoods depleted (median log₂FC = −0.98) — the strongest single-cell signal in the study.

Methods — at a glance

Cohort

28 donors — 21 CIDP (EAN/PNS 2021 criteria, treatment-naive at first visit) and 7 neurological disease controls (NPH, CMT4C, anti-MAG, diabetic neuropathy, IIH). Age 46–86. Age and sex included as covariates.

Single-cell RNA-seq

PBMCs isolated by Ficoll density gradient. Libraries prepared with 10x Genomics Chromium 3' v3 and sequenced on Illumina NovaSeq 6000. Cell Ranger for alignment; Scanpy for QC (200–6,000 genes/cell, <10% mito, ScDblFinder doublet removal). 327,559 cells retained.

Integration and annotation

scVI batch integration with donor and batch as covariates → 50-dim latent space used for KNN graph, UMAP, and Milo. Cell type annotation performed on integrated latent space.

Differential abundance

Milo (graph-based DA) on scVI latent KNN. Spatial FDR < 0.10. Age + sex as covariates. Neighborhood-level testing → 193 DA neighborhoods across 11 cell types.

Differential expression + GSEA

Wilcoxon DEG comparing cells in DA neighborhoods to cells in non-significant neighborhoods of the same cell type. GSEA across MSigDB Hallmark, Reactome, GO:BP, and C7 (Immunologic) collections.

Proteomics

Olink Explore HT — 3,255 plasma proteins by proximity extension assay. Empirical Bayes moderation, BH adjustment. Same age/sex covariates. Gene-protein concordance, pathway GSEA comparison, hypothesis-driven validation, cell-type deconvolution.
